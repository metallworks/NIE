How building works

Your coaster starts as a station. Press + Add to lay a new segment, then shape it with the four knobs next to it.

PitchTilts the nose up (+) or down (−). Keep adding pitch-up segments and you get a loop. YawTurns right (+) or left (−). Turns stay level even when the track is banked. BankRolls the track right (+) or left (−) around the rider's heartline. ScaleThe segment length in meters. The same angle over a longer segment is a gentler curve.

Drag a knob sideways or up and down to change it. Tap a knob to type an exact value, and double-click it to set it back to zero.

  • Segment Type switches between Normal, Chain, Launch and Brake. Each has its own speed settings.
  • Bank Mode: Rel. Bank adds the bank to the previous segment. Abs. Bank sets the exact bank angle at the end of the segment, where 0 is upright and 180 is upside down.
  • Circuit joins the last segment back to the station. Point the end of your track roughly toward the station for a smooth join.

Keys: WS pitch, AD yaw, QE bank, RF scale (hold Shift for ×5), Enter add, [] select, Space ride. Click any segment in the 3D view to select it. Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
